FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment by entering your unique tracking number on our "Track Shipment" page. We provide real-time updates via GPS and status notifications.

What countries do you ship to?

We operate in over 120 countries worldwide. Whether it's domestic delivery or international freight, our network covers major global trade routes.

Do you offer insurance for cargo?

Yes, we offer comprehensive cargo insurance options to protect your goods against loss or damage during transit. You can add this during the quote process.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (Full Container Load) means you rent the entire container. LCL (Less than Container Load) means your goods share a container with other shipments, which is cost-effective for smaller loads.

How can I get a shipping quote?

You can use our online "Get Quote" form by providing details about weight, dimensions, origin, and destination. Alternatively, contact our sales team directly.

Do you handle hazardous materials?

Yes, we are certified to handle hazardous materials (HAZMAT) with strict adherence to international safety regulations and proper documentation.
